Flexler showcase assets

This public Dapper Dan feature includes the owner-approved Flexler workbench,
its native material layers, and its semantic resource values.

The paired header uses the six-ring spring artwork beside the FleXler wordmark.
The standalone Fl mark is used in the workbench drawers. The capital X in the
wordmark joins a straight and a flexed metal bar. These original Flexler brand
exports and the raw/polished labradorite textures were created with ImageGen
and are included unchanged with the owner's approval.

Instrument Sans Regular/SemiBold and Geist Mono Medium accompany their OFL
license texts. Microsoft-MIT.txt preserves the notice for the adapted MAUI
native input code. OpenSans-Apache-2.0.txt accompanies Dapper Dan's existing
Open Sans fonts. Optional mechanical feedback paths are scoped to this feature;
no sound clips are packaged, preserving the source workbench's silent fallback.

This showcase does not replace Dapper Dan's app icon, splash, bundle identity,
platform entry points, signing configuration, or local Keiki database.
